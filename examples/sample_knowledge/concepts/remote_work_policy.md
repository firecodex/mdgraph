# Remote Work Hardware Allowance

Employees receive a yearly stipend for home office setup managed by the [IT Department](./it_department.md).

## Approved Purchases
All equipment must be sourced via the [Vendor Portal](./vendor_portal.md).
Eligible hardware includes ergonomic monitors, mechanical keyboards, and noise-canceling headsets.

## Security Compliance
Any connected devices must comply with the [Security Guidelines](./security_guidelines.md).
