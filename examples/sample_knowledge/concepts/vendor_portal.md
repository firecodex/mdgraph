# Enterprise Vendor Portal

The Vendor Portal is an internal procurement tool connecting employees to authorized hardware distributors.

## Integration
Maintained by the [IT Department](./it_department.md) under budget approved by Finance.
Orders submitted under [Remote Work Policy](./remote_work_policy.md) are auto-approved up to $1,500.
